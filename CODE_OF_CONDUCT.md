# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want everyone to feel welcome in this project, whatever their background, experience level, identity or location. Contributors and maintainers pledge to make taking part a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person, and say it so it can be acted on.
- Accept feedback gracefully and assume good intent.
- Respect other people's time: keep issues and pull requests focused.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory jokes or language.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Anything else that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, commits) and in any other space where someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may temporarily or permanently block anyone who does.

To report a problem, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment, or contact the maintainer through the profile at <https://github.com/Vigesh-VS>. Reports are handled privately.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the ideas in the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
